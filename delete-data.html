Delete your Lugma data

Lugma by Hamza Hashem has no user accounts. Everything Lugma stores is kept on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time, without contacting us.

How to delete your data in the app

  1. Open Lugma and tap the Settings (gear) button.
  2. Open Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Reset all local data and confirm. This deletes everything listed below.
  4. Or delete one type at a time: Clear search history, Clear saved locations, Clear favourites and savings, Clear provider website data, or Remove key (under AI).

Uninstalling Lugma also deletes all of its data from your phone.

Data that is deleted

Recent dish and restaurant searches, chosen and recent delivery locations, favourites, remembered prices, savings estimates, your OpenRouter key, your settings, support-prompt preferences, and cookies, cache and storage from Talabat, Deliveroo and Noon Food websites. Deletion is immediate and permanent. Lugma data is excluded from Android cloud backup, so no copy is kept elsewhere.

Data that is kept

Google Play keeps records of any optional support purchase under Google's own policies; Lugma cannot delete them. Talabat, Deliveroo, Noon Food, OpenStreetMap and OpenRouter keep data they received under their own privacy policies; contact them directly to ask for deletion.

Feedback emails

If you sent us feedback by email, ask us to delete it by emailing feedback@alchemistiq.com from the same address with the subject "Delete my data". We delete those emails within 30 days of your request.
