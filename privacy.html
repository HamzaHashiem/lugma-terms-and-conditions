Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-04

Who we are

Lugma is developed by AlchemistIQ. Contact us at feedback@alchemistiq.com. Lugma has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and AlchemistIQ does not run a server that receives your searches.

What stays on your device

Matching and price comparison run on your device. Lugma stores your OpenRouter key (if you add one), your settings, up to 6 recent searches, up to 5 recent delivery locations, and cookies, cache and storage created by provider websites. The key and preferences are kept in Android secure storage. This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Delivery providers

To show current menus and prices, the app contacts Talabat, Deliveroo and Noon Food directly. It sends your search text and your delivery location (coordinates or an area code derived from them), together with information any website receives, such as your IP address, device and browser details, and cookies those sites set. Dish images are loaded from the providers and their content delivery networks. When you open a dish, its link is passed to the provider's app or your browser. Each provider's own privacy policy applies to the data it receives.

Place search and location names

When you search for a place, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. When you use your current location, its coordinates are sent there to find a readable place name. Location permission is requested only when you choose to use your current location, and location is never collected in the background. You can always choose an area manually instead.

Optional AI matching

AI matching is used only if you add your own OpenRouter API key and keep AI turned on. Your search and the names of candidate dishes and restaurants are then sent directly to OpenRouter, which may pass them to the model provider you select. Your key is sent to OpenRouter over HTTPS to authenticate. OpenRouter and model providers have their own logging and retention policies. Your search text may itself contain personal information, so avoid typing details you would not want to share.

Feedback

Feedback is sent only if you choose to send an email from your email app. Your email provider and our inbox receive your email address, your message and, if you choose, the app and Android version. We keep feedback only as long as needed to respond and improve Lugma.

Your choices

In Settings > Privacy & data you can clear search history, clear saved locations, clear provider website data, remove your OpenRouter key, or reset all local data. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. To ask about feedback you sent, contact feedback@alchemistiq.com.

Security

Network connections use HTTPS with normal certificate checks. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Lugma is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and show the new version and date here.
